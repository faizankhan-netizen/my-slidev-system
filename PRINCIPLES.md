# Strategic Presentation Principles 🧠
*A Framework for Intelligent Context-Aware Design — v3.0 (Cinematic V5)*

This document defines the methodology for transforming a raw topic into a premium, audience-resonant Slidev presentation using the **Cinematic Engine V5**.

---

## 1. Phase 1: Context Discovery (Auto-Resolution)
With the V5 Engine, manual selection is secondary to **Topic Intelligence**. The engine analyzes your input across the **Triad of Context**:

### A. The Audience (Auto-Boost)
- **Learners/Kids**: Boosts `style-school` for high-energy, tactile variety.
- **Developers/Tech**: Boosts `style-cyber` for command-line aesthetics.
- **Executives**: Boosts `style-business` or `style-editorial` for high-end density.

### B. The Subject (Signal Detection)
- The `TopicAnalyzer` scans for signals like "Legacy" (Luxury), "System" (Cyber), "Nature" (Eco), or "Lesson" (School).

### C. The Tone (Energy Bias)
- **Refined/Elegant**: Biases towards serif fonts and minimalist layouts.
- **Playful/Bold**: Biases towards vibrant palettes and hand-drawn accents.

---

## 2. Phase 2: Narrative Engineering (The Energy Arc)
Don't just list slides. Design the **Energy Arc** using the `ArcComposer`:

1. **The Hook (Cover)**: High-energy, cinematic, wide-focus.
2. **The Context (Quote/Intro)**: Calm energy, focus on meaning, low visual density.
3. **The Body (Modules)**: Standard energy, structured information, repeating patterns.
4. **The Climax (Data/Media)**: High-energy, intense visuals, "the reveal."
5. **The Reflection (Concept)**: Calm energy, summary of learning, spacious layout.
6. **The Closer (Finale)**: High-energy, call to action, memorable visual.

---

## 3. Phase 3: Design Sovereignty & Scholarly Rigor
To maintain "Design Sovereignty," follow these strict implementation rules:

1. **Soul in CSS**: Never use inline `style=""` for visual properties. Only use semantic class names.
2. **Scholarly Rigor**: For academic decks, prioritize **Verticality** over **Grids**. Long hypotheses require full-width breathing room to maintain logical flow.
3. **Minimalist Anchor**: Academic trust is built on high contrast and low decoration. Remove "cutesy" default icons (sparkles) in favor of structural numbering.
4. **Vibrant Variety**: For educational archetypes (School/Workshop), ensure the `bg_palette` is active to prevent audience "Design Fatigue."
5. **Luminance Safety**: Trust the orchestrator to handle Dark/Light text contrast based on the background saturation.
6. **The "Breather" Rule**: Never place three text-heavy slides in a row. The engine automatically injects a `media_focus` or `quote` slide to reset the audience's attention span.

---

## 4. The Visual Quality Checklist (V5)
- [ ] **Auto-Resolved**: Did the `TopicAnalyzer` pick the right archetype for the subject?
- [ ] **Arc Compliant**: Does the energy flow from Hook → Climax → Closer?
- [ ] **Texture Active**: Do the backgrounds rotate per slide (Lined → Grid → Dots)?
- [ ] **Class-Native**: Are all components using CSS classes instead of inline styles?
- [ ] **BoundGuard Verified**: No text bleed, no overlapping infographics.
